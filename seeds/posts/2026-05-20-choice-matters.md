**Choice Matters 選擇很重要** 

Notes from a mediocre talk I attended recently:
1. **Choice matters.** Pick a challenging yet genuinely intriguing problem to work on. 挑有興趣的大問題。
2. **Don't chase trends.** Only pursue problems you care about deeply enough to work on **every single day**. 不追潮流，但求能每日堅持。
3. **Never attribute my success to my own judgment or effort while blaming others' success on luck or opportunism.** Just don't be a double-standard douchebag. 勿當雙標仔。
4. **Don't rush. It’s okay to look around if you don’t know what to solve yet.** When there is no problem to tackle, practice being an excellent observer. 沒有問題也沒問題，觀察這個世界吧！

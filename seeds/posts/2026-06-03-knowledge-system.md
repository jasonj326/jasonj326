English version below

回覆讀友手札：

我覺得是每個人的大腦有不同的偏好，我自己就喜歡有系統的結構，但身邊大多數的人覺得很無趣。

目前我自創的分類是四個層次：

Layer 1: 剛性限制、第一性原理。所有逆天的東西都必然失敗，目前我們知道的是物理學、熱力學、生物限制等。但第一性原理也有時代的局限，三百年前沒有細菌的概念，想必我們現在也有些概念是不清楚或錯誤的，但沒辦法，就是時代的局限。

Layer 2: 權力結構 (e.g., 貧富差距、九大核武國、產油國、半導體產業)、文化限制、認知限制、大腦侷限與錯覺。這邊的定義是，人類很難跨越，但有機會透過學習、訓練，去克服我們的偏見與權力結構。

Layer 3: 方法論：邏輯思辨、科學方法與非科學方法、貝葉斯思維、語言與現實。這一層是方法論，連結 Layer 2 & 4，任何可以幫助我們更好學一門技藝、一門專業的方法論，都會被我歸納在這裡。

Layer 4: 應用：世界上所有的應用，從做一道菜、炒飯，到審閱合約的 SOP ，到經營律師事務所，從讀書考試拿高分，到直播賣貨、到怎麼跟陌生人對話、用 AI 寫程式。這邊都是一般人在學習的東西，最快變現，但也最快被時代取代。

我喜歡自己弄這些架構，會讓我對世界多一點認識與控制（的錯覺），甚至有我掌握 L1-L3 世界底層邏輯的成就感（的幻覺），所以我也在 L4 邁進，多做一點應用，多賺一點錢，早日 FIRE 上岸，我就可以專心去耽溺於 L1-L3 了 (笑）



Since late last year, I've been optimizing my knowledge system. Sharing it publicly through readers' questions turned out to be a positive outcome, so I've reposted it here and translated it into English.

Replying to a reader's comment:

My observation is that everyone's brain is wired differently and thus has different preferences. I personally enjoy structured systems, but most people around me find them boring or unnecessarily demanding.

My current knowledge system consists of four layers:

Layer 1: Rigid Constraints & First Principles. Anything that defies these will inevitably fail. Currently, we know this includes physics, thermodynamics, and biological limitations. However, first principles also have era-specific limitations. Three hundred years ago, the concept of bacteria/Pathogen didn't exist; undoubtedly, there are concepts we hold today that are unclear or incorrect. Nevertheless, we are bound by the limitations of our times.

Layer 2: Power Structures (e.g., wealth disparity, the nine nuclear-armed states, oil-producing nations, the semiconductor industry), Cultural Constraints, Cognitive Limitations, and Brain Biases/Illusions. The definition here is that these are boundaries humans find difficult to cross, though there is potential to overcome our biases and power structures through learning and training.

Layer 3: Methodology. This includes logical reasoning, the scientific method and non-scientific methods, Bayesian thinking, and the relationship between language and reality. This layer serves as the methodology connecting Layer 2 and Layer 4. Any approach that helps us better master a craft or profession is categorized here.

Layer 4: Application. This encompasses all applications in the world: from cooking a dish or fried rice, to the SOP for reviewing contracts, to running a law firm, from studying to ace exams, to live-streaming sales, to talking to strangers, and using AI to write code. These are the things  people learn on a daily basis; they offer the quickest path to monetization but are also the quickest to be replaced by the times.

I enjoy creating these frameworks because it gives me a sense of understanding and control (an illusion) over the world. There is even a sense of achievement (a delusion) in feeling that I grasp the underlying logic of the world from L1 to L3. Therefore, I am also advancing in L4, focusing on more applications to earn more money. My goal is to achieve FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) as soon as possible, so I can then dedicate myself fully to indulging in L1–L3 (LOL).

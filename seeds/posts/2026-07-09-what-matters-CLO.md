I have been asking GCs and CLOs (and other senior legal professionals) this question: What makes a good GC or CLO?

Here are the answers I’ve received so far:
1. Trust your team and delegate tasks properly.
2. Build strong relationships with stakeholders (including regulators, investors and the board, and even competitors).
3. Cultivate a tolerance for team errors so team members can learn and grow, ultimately strengthening the whole team.

Glad to see Coinbase CLO Paul Grewal's take on this
> 1. Talent magnet. Who a CLO can attract and develop is maybe the most important talent they can bring to an Exec team.  
> 2. Strategic and selective voice. Does your top lawyer have a demonstrated knack for knowing to butt in on a strategic conversation at the company, and when to shut up?  
> 3. Let 'em cook. The CLOs I most admire know how to coach up their CEO and others on public and community communications without killing their personality and spirit.

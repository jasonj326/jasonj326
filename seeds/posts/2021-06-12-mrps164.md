6月13日，No. 164，近義詞：引誘

今天週日、有貓，還不按愛心嗎？
沒錯，我在引誘你

LURE, ENTICE, INVEIGLE, DECOY, TEMPT, SEDUCE mean to lead astray from one's true course.

#LURE implies a drawing into danger, evil, or difficulty through attracting and deceiving. The mastermind of the Ponzi scheme lured naive investors with get-rich-quick schemes.
#ENTICE suggests drawing by artful or adroit means. Dark pattern is arguably designed to entice new customers in their decision-making process.

#INVEIGLE implies enticing by cajoling or flattering. fund-raisers inveigled wealthy 

#DECOY implies a luring into entrapment by artifice. The credit collector decoyed the old lady into a legal trap.

#TEMPT implies the presenting of an attraction so strong that it overcomes the restraints of conscience or better judgment. Lawyers should not be tempted by the offer of money to engage unethical practice, even though most junior lawyers incur huge debt from their student loans.

#SEDUCE implies a leading astray by persuasion or false promises. Inexperienced consumers are likely seduced by the salesperson's oral assurances.

這個版本的重點

建立數位資產監管分工。這份 2026 年 9 月 10 日公布的《CLARITY Act》參議院替代草案，擬區分代幣本身與募資時使用的投資契約，由 SEC 負責相關證券發行及資訊揭露，並由 CFTC 監管受規範的數位商品現貨交易與中介機構；證券代幣化本身不會改變其證券性質。 §10102 · §10505 · §20201

提供合規途徑，同時加強客戶保護。草案設計附條件的募資豁免、定期揭露及關係人出售限制，要求交易所、經紀商、交易商與合格保管機構遵守登記、資本、利益衝突及客戶資產隔離等規範，並補強破產時的客戶財產處理；一般數位商品不因此取得 SIPC 證券保障。 §10103–10104 · §10110 · §20204–20206

以實際控制判斷 DeFi 責任。草案保護特定不具控制權的開發、驗證及自我保管活動，同時要求實際控制非去中心化交易系統的人依其業務接受登記、反洗錢及制裁規範；另增設反詐騙、網路安全、可疑交易暫時凍結與跨機構執法工具，並禁止僅因錢包不活動就將合法自我保管資產視為無主財產。 §10301 · §10305 · §20209 · §20216

限制穩定幣被動收益，保留特定活動獎勵。草案禁止僅因持有支付型穩定幣而支付利息或類似銀行存款的收益，但容許符合條件、與真實活動有關的獎勵，並修正 GENIUS Act 的信用合作社待遇、保管及執行命令等規則。 §10404 · §11004

公職倫理條款有明確期限。草案限制特定公職人員、員工及配偶在任職期間有償發行或贊助數位資產，設有例外，且僅由司法部長提起相關民事執法；該禁令於 2029 年 1 月 20 日中午失效，條文並明訂其後不得追究該條責任，即使涉及失效前的行為。全案一般於立法後 360 日生效，須訂規則的條款可能更晚，部分另有期限。 §30101 · §30105 · §40101

Disclosure requirements for certain transactions involving ancillary assets

Special disposition restrictions by related persons

Requires SEC rules governing coordinated control and related-person token sales, including holding periods and resale limits, a normally 90-day non-control certification process, ownership and transaction reporting, tailored exemptions, and recovery of profits from prohibited sales.

Characteristics of network tokens

Requires SEC rules recognizing qualifying network-derived economic and governance rights without disqualifying a network token, preserves specified final judicial determinations, and excludes qualifying tokens underlying exchange-traded products as of January 1, 2026 from ancillary-asset and securities treatment.

Exemptive authority

Insider trading with respect to ancillary asset transactions

Applies existing securities insider-trading rules and remedies to securities transactions involving ancillary assets, requires rules including a defense for qualifying prearranged trading plans, and excludes secondary transactions that are not otherwise securities transactions.

§ 20218

Additional registrations

Permits SEC-registered brokers, dealers, exchanges, and their affiliates, including alternative trading systems, to obtain applicable CFTC registrations, subject to notice to the SEC.

§ 20219

Eligibility of alternative trading systems

Prevents denial of an alternative trading system’s exchange exemption solely because it also trades digital commodities or payment stablecoins, and clarifies when such systems and digital commodity exchanges are excluded from the definition of a national securities exchange facility.

Sections 30101–30106

Division C · Ethics requirements

§ 30101

Ban on certain digital asset transactions

Prohibits covered public officials, employees, and spouses from issuing or sponsoring digital assets for consideration during covered service, bars exchanges from listing assets issued in violation, provides specified exceptions, and assigns exclusive civil enforcement to the Attorney General with disgorgement and monetary penalties.

Source · p. 617 ↗
§ 30102

Ethics requirements relating to digital assets

Expands federal ethics disclosures to cover specified remunerated digital asset interests and transactions, including qualifying interests exceeding $1,000 at year-end and applicable spouse and dependent-child reporting.

Source · p. 626 ↗
§ 30103

GAO report to Congress

Requires the Government Accountability Office to review digital asset ethics restrictions and their enforcement and recommend legislative or administrative improvements within 360 days after enactment.

Source · p. 627 ↗
§ 30104

Effective date

Makes Division C effective on the earlier of 360 days after enactment or 60 days after the final rule under section 10102(b), and limits its application to conduct occurring on or after that effective date.

Source · p. 628 ↗
§ 30105

Sunset

Terminates section 30101’s prohibitions at noon on January 20, 2029, and expressly prevents liability or penalties under that section after termination even for conduct occurring before the sunset.

Source · p. 629 ↗
§ 30106

Severability

Preserves the remaining provisions of Division C and their valid applications if a provision or application is held unconstitutional.

Division D · Effective date

§ 40101

Effective date

Generally makes the Act effective 360 days after enactment, but delays provisions requiring rulemaking until the later of that date or 60 days after the applicable final rule, subject to specifically stated exceptions.
